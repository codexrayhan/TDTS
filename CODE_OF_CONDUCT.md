# TDTS Code of Conduct

TDTS is committed to a welcoming, safe, and productive community for everyone. This policy is adapted from the principles of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) and is intended to set clear expectations for project spaces.

## Our commitment

Contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and community members are expected to help create an environment where participation is respectful regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, socioeconomic status, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Examples of constructive participation include:

- Showing empathy, patience, and respect in technical discussions.
- Giving actionable feedback about code or ideas rather than attacking people.
- Accepting reasonable feedback and corrections professionally.
- Making room for different viewpoints, backgrounds, and experience levels.
- Prioritizing the health of the project and community over personal conflict.

## Unacceptable behavior

The project does not tolerate harassment or conduct that makes participation unsafe. This includes, but is not limited to:

- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, threats, intimidation, trolling, or sustained personal attacks.
- Harassment in public or private project channels.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of collaboration, review, issue triage, or community spaces.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional community.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this policy consistently. They may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate these expectations, and they may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Maintainers should explain moderation decisions when doing so is appropriate and safe, while protecting the privacy of people who report concerns.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository spaces and in public situations where someone is representing TDTS or its community, including official communication channels and project events.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `noreply@tdts.dev`. Do not open a public issue for sensitive reports.

Reports should be reviewed promptly, fairly, and with appropriate confidentiality. People responsible for reviewing a report should disclose conflicts of interest and step aside when necessary.

Possible responses may include a private correction, a formal warning, temporary limits on participation, or permanent removal from project spaces, depending on severity, context, and repeated behavior.
